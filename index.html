A call to action

The bank of time we used to have is getting over.

This is not a message about dates. It is a message about readiness — for His coming, and for our own departure.

Read the full message

To a great extent, the church community tends to believe that there is still a bank of time — as it has been for the past decades or centuries before our Lord Jesus comes to take His church.

This belief, perception or mindset of no urgency; this “as usual” mentality is likely going to see many Christians left behind and surprised when Jesus comes to take His church. This lack of readiness and relaxation appears to be the current situation with most believers around the globe, yet the time of the end has already begun.

“I need to shake up everyone to this truth… This is a call to action.”
