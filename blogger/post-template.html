The bank of time we used to have is getting over. This is not a message about dates — it is a call to wake up, watch and be ready.

For too long the church has lived with the quiet assumption that there is still plenty of time. That “as usual” mentality is the very thing that can leave a believer unprepared for the coming of the Lord.

The time of the end has already begun

Jesus did not tell us to predict the day. He told us to watch. The signs He spoke about are not given to satisfy our curiosity; they are given to stir our readiness.

“Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh.” Matthew 25:13

What readiness looks like

  • Watch — discern the signs and stay awake spiritually.
  • Prepare — guard your heart and keep your walk with God current.
  • Be ready — live today as though His coming could be today.

Readiness is not fear. It is the quiet confidence of a life that is already turned toward Him. It is the oil in the lamp, the prayer that does not stop, and the love that keeps working while we wait.

A word to carry with you

Whatever season you are walking through, do not go back to sleep. Wake someone else with the hope you have found. Share the message. Open a book. Listen to a teaching. Let the Word do its work.

Keep watching. Keep preparing.
