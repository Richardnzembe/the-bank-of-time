About this watch

This is a call to action.

A whistle-blower’s cry to a church that has grown comfortable with time — and a plea to watch, prepare and be ready.

“And when these things begin to come to pass, look up and lift up your heads, for your redemption draws near.”

Luke 21:28

Church, take heed and do not return to sleep. Watch now!

To a great extent, the church community tends to believe that there is still a bank of time — as it has been for the past decades or centuries before our Lord Jesus comes to take His church.

This belief, perception or mindset of no urgency; this “as usual” mentality is likely going to see many Christians left behind and surprised when Jesus comes to take His church. This lack of readiness and relaxation appears to be the current situation with most believers around the globe, yet the time of the end has already begun.

The time of the end has already begun

I need to shake up everyone to this truth. Of course, yes — I made this end-time website to try and achieve that goal. This is a call to action.

Brethren, ladies and gentlemen, we have tended to live in the normalcy of time with less urgency for preparation of the return of our Lord Jesus for too long.

A new thing, and children of light

In 2026 I am beginning with a call to action. That bank of time we used to have in the years gone by is getting over and making its own history. A fulfilment of a mystery of a lifetime is about to take place, possibly during our lifetime. God is about to declare:

“Behold, I am doing a new thing.” Isaiah 43:19

But 1 Thessalonians 5 tells us that we are children of light and therefore that day cannot come upon us as a thief. We watch for signs which our Lord Jesus talked about, and right now those signs are manifesting.

Signs to watch for

Jesus talked of signs to watch out for in order to prepare and get ready, so that people are not caught unaware. Sadly, these happen to be spiritual sayings which are hardly understood fully by one who is not well consecrated in the word of God. Jesus talked about taking note of signs of the end as indicating watchfulness.

Readiness for departure

My cry right now is to wake someone up and help arouse their spirit to this reality — for their awareness, preparation and readiness for the coming of the Lord for His church. However, even if Jesus does not come in your lifetime, readiness for His coming is always critical. We all know that everyone’s day of departure, of going to the Lord, is hidden.

The matter therefore is about readiness for your departure, or His coming for your redemption.

A whistle-blower’s duty

My duty right now therefore is to act as a whistle-blower — to help awaken the church and everyone else to watching out for the coming of the Lord.
